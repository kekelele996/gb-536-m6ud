## Design Context

### Users

Enterprise NLP data managers, annotators, adjudicators, and auditors working in a focused internal quality workflow. They need to compare evidence quickly, understand why agreement changed, resolve disagreements without exposing sensitive corpus text, and retain an accountable decision trail.

### Brand Personality

Restrained, evidence-led, trustworthy. The product should feel like a precise editorial workbench: calm during routine review, unmistakably cautionary when disagreement or policy conflict needs attention, and never promotional.

### Aesthetic Direction

A light annotation console built on cool neutral surfaces, ink-like text, and a limited amber accent reserved for disagreement and pending judgment. Structure comes from rules, compact tables, comparison lanes, and clear typographic hierarchy rather than card stacks. Status always combines icon, label, and shape; color is never the sole signal. Avoid neon, gradients, glass effects, oversized marketing type, decorative metrics, and generic dashboard tiles.

### Design Principles

1. Put evidence before summary: show sample size, observed agreement, chance agreement, applicability, and diff evidence together.
2. Preserve reading flow: keep comparison columns aligned, actions close to the evidence they affect, and dense information scannable.
3. Make uncertainty explicit: use amber only for unresolved disagreement, and pair every state with text and an icon.
4. Protect source content: display masked excerpts and structural labels, never sensitive raw corpus text in logs or audit views.
5. Meet WCAG AA contrast, keyboard focus, reduced-motion preferences, and responsive layouts without removing critical actions.
